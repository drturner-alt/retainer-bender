Open or drop the lower-arch STL from iTero

3-3 retainer picks

Drag to rotate, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Click on the tooth to add a point. Pick in order from one end of the wire to the other:

  1. 43, distal end of the bond area
  2. 43, mesial end of the bond area
  3. 42, 41, 31, 32: where the wire should touch, about two-thirds up the lingual surface
  4. 33, mesial end, then distal end of the bond area

Add a second point on an incisor if the wire should hug its whole lingual surface.
